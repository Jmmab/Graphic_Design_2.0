    - through `Image menu` x through `Adjustments Panel` (** works differently)
        <!-- first option creates a "nested" filter, while the second creates its separate layer -->
    - **Brightness/Contrast**
        - Brightness: increases or decreases the highlights or the shadows of the image
        - Contrast: controls the levels simultaneously -> the bight areas get brighter and the dark areas get darker
            > High contrasting images tend to look artificial
    - **Levels**
        - It's meant to adjust the tonal range of the image by adjusting the _intensity levels_ of the highlights, shadows and midtones
        - Black, White, and Grey sliders
    - **Curves**
        - Curves do the same things as Levels, but with more control
    - **Exposure**
        - Basically, Exposure is a measurement of the amount of ***light*** an image has been _exposed_ to
            <!-- underexposed x overexposed  -->
        - Has a very drastic effect `!`
        - Offset and Gamma correction
            <!-- a little bit more advanced, not really essential -->
    - **Vibrance**
        - Vibrance x Saturation: Saturation increases or decreases the intensity (Hue) of all the color in an image, while the Vibrance is more selective
    - **Hue/Saturation**
        - Literally changes colors
            - 'Colorize'
    - **Color Balance**
        - Allows you to make adjustments to color more selectively
        - Very commonly used to correct color imperfections
        - Also used for _Compositing_, when intentionally trying to create a desired effect. E.g. : "warm" and "cold"
    - **Black & White**
        - Gives you a very nice level of control over the blacks and white (and previously colored) pixels of an image, which allows you to isolate and mess with parts of the image
            <!-- you can even add tints -->
    - Photo Filter
        - A super quick way of applying filters, specially built-in ones
